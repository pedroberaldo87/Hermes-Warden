Usage: bw share [options] <id> <organizationId> [encodedJson]

--DEPRECATED-- Move an item to an organization.

Arguments:
  id              Object's globally unique `id`.
  organizationId  Organization's globally unique `id`.
  encodedJson     Encoded json of an array of collection ids. Can also be piped
                  into stdin.

Options:
  -h, --help      display help for command

  Examples:

    bw share 4af958ce-96a7-45d9-beed-1e70fabaa27a 6d82949b-b44d-468a-adae-3f3bacb0ea32 WyI5NzQwNTNkMC0zYjMzLTRiOTgtODg2ZS1mZWNmNWM4ZGJhOTYiXQ==
    echo '["974053d0-3b33-4b98-886e-fecf5c8dba96"]' | bw encode | bw share 4af958ce-96a7-45d9-beed-1e70fabaa27a 6d82949b-b44d-468a-adae-3f3bacb0ea32
